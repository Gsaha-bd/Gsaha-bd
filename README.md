## About

I am an environmental researcher and geospatial analyst specializing in satellite remote sensing, catchment-scale flood risk modeling, and climate hazard assessment. Currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Global Change Geography at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, I bridge Earth observation data with practical environmental risk management.  

My background spans academic hydrological research and international development policy. I have published peer-reviewed research on GIS-based flash flood risk zonation in the Arabian Journal of Geosciences and delivered spatial evidence for climate adaptation initiatives.
